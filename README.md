SOLANO HEAT — a complete original 3D open-world crime game in the sun-soaked coastal city of Port Solano: 15 story missions, a 5-star police dispatch with roadblocks and the CONDOR-1 helicopter, 5 weapons, a garage with upgrades, respect ranks, 3 street races, a crew of 3 with abilities, shops, 2 safehouses, 4 radio stations and saves. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

## Play

WASD move / drive · E enter and steal cars · Mouse aim and shoot · Q weapon wheel · 1–5 weapons · R radio · Z / H / C crew abilities · Shift sprint · Space jump / handbrake · L save · P pause.
